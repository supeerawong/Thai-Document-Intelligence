# Code of Conduct

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone. Be respectful, assume good intent, accept constructive feedback, and keep technical discussions focused on the work.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and sustained disruption. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban participants whose behavior is harmful to the community.
